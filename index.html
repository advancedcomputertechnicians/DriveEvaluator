Privacy Policy for DriveEvaluator

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Disclaimer: DriveEvaluator is an independent educational training simulator developed by Advanced Computer Technicians Inc. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Ministry of Transportation of Ontario (MTO), DriveTest, or any government agency.

1. Introduction

Advanced Computer Technicians Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") provides DriveEvaluator as an educational mock test simulator. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled within the application.

2. Information Collection and Storage

DriveEvaluator is designed with a privacy-first model:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide personal credentials to use the application.
  • Local Storage Only: All test results, demerit calculations, scoring notes, and progress logs are saved locally on your device storage. We do not transmit or host your evaluation records on external servers.
  • No Background Tracking: The application does not collect precise GPS tracking, biometric data, or microphone audio.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties or data brokers. The app does not integrate third-party advertising networks or user-tracking analytics SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

DriveEvaluator is intended for learner drivers preparing for official road examinations (typically ages 16 and older). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated modification date.
